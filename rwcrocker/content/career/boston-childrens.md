## Summary
For two years, I was a Research Assistant in the Vascular Biology lab of Dr. Timothy Hla at Boston Children's Hospital.

## Institution

[Boston Children's Hospital](https://www.childrenshospital.org/) is a leading pediatric medical center. Its partnership with [Harvard Medical School](https://hms.harvard.edu/) makes for a strong research environment.

## Responsibilities

I did both bench work and bioinformatics, but I became the lab's main bioinformatics person. Most of my time went to building pipelines to clean and analyze single-cell RNA sequencing data in models of vascular and immune disease.

My work contributed to two publications:

* Niaudet et al., *EMBO Molecular Medicine*, 2023
* Lin et al., *eLife*, 2026
